Shattered Veil #

This is my first post as I write Shattered Veil, a science fiction work about a distant colonly established by the EU but failed to fully establish. The colony is currently a high medieval soceity with the terraforming and colonizing machinery in self-imposed isolation to prevent dependence.

Lady Rebeca Oxtoa #

Lady Rebeca Oxtoa, Vibaron Vitoria is a young woman, about 18, whos is discerning her future at the Abby of Hagenau. She must chose between an prepetually unmarried Nun to tend to Sutinir, the maintainer of the system.

Kertesz’s Speaker #

A man from the margins of the colony who has formed a pact with Ker the speaker gets the ability to control Nettoyant’s cleansing fire in exchange for acting as its agent to remove Weg from the voting, as Kertesz wins any tie. This leads us to…

Auxil, the engine of Colonization #

Auxil is the triple redundant AI Computer that manages colonizaiton with three core AIs: Maor, which manages life, Kertesz, which manages terraforming, and Weg, which manages the infrastrucutre. They each have three agents, but that’s a discussion for a later blog post.
